# 👥 HR Analytics & Employee Performance Dashboard

## 📊 Overview

The HR Analytics & Employee Performance Dashboard is an interactive Power BI solution designed to help Human Resource professionals and business leaders gain valuable insights into employee performance, workforce retention, compensation trends, turnover rates, and attrition risks.

The dashboard consolidates workforce data into a centralized reporting platform that supports data-driven decision-making, enabling organizations to identify high-performing employees, understand turnover patterns, optimize compensation strategies, and improve workforce planning.

This project demonstrates advanced Power BI capabilities, including data modeling, DAX calculations, KPI development, workforce analytics, and interactive dashboard design.

---

## 🎯 Business Objective

Organizations often face challenges in understanding employee performance, retention, and workforce trends. HR teams require reliable insights to reduce turnover, improve employee engagement, and support strategic talent management.

This dashboard was developed to answer key business questions:

* Which departments experience the highest turnover?
* Who are the longest-serving employees?
* What are the major reasons employees leave?
* Which employees are at risk of attrition?
* How does performance vary across departments?
* Is there a relationship between salary and employee performance?
* What is the total salary expenditure across the organization?
* Which departments demonstrate the strongest workforce stability?

## 📋 Dashboard Structure

### Page 1: Employee Retention & Turnover Analysis

This page focuses on workforce retention, employee tenure, turnover trends, and risk assessment.

### Key Visualizations

#### Top 5 Longest Serving Employees

Displays employees with the highest tenure within the organization, highlighting workforce stability and long-term employee commitment.

#### Top 5 Employees by Days Served

Ranks employees according to the total number of days served within the organization.

#### Employee Exit Analysis

Breaks down employee departures by reason:

* Personal Reasons
* Found Another Job
* Career Growth Opportunities
* Retirement

#### Turnover Rate by Department

Analyzes employee turnover across departments, helping management identify areas requiring retention initiatives.

#### Employee Risk Assessment Table

Identifies employees based on performance scores and attrition risk levels:

* High Risk
* Medium Risk
* Low Risk

### Key Insights

* Marketing recorded the highest employee turnover rate.
* Engineering experienced the second-highest turnover rate.
* Personal reasons accounted for the largest proportion of employee exits.
* Several employees demonstrated over ten years of service.
* Risk assessment helps HR teams proactively address retention challenges.

## 📈 Page 2: Employee Performance & Compensation Analysis

This page focuses on workforce productivity, compensation, and performance metrics.

### Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

| KPI                       | Value  |
| ------------------------- | ------ |
| Total Salary Expenses     | £4.85M |
| Employees Left            | 28     |
| High Performing Employees | 26     |

### Key Visualizations

#### Employee Performance Distribution

Compares employees performing above and below the performance threshold.

#### Performance Score by Department

Evaluates departmental performance using employee performance scores.

#### Average Performance Score by Department

Measures the overall effectiveness of employees within each department.

#### Salary by Job Title

Provides insights into compensation distribution across organizational roles.

#### Salary-Performance Correlation

Analyzes the relationship between employee salaries and performance levels by department.

### Key Insights

* Marketing and Engineering demonstrated strong overall performance.
* HR exhibited the strongest positive relationship between salary and performance.
* Engineering showed minimal correlation between salary and performance.
* Sales Representatives earned the highest average salary.
* High-performing employees significantly outnumbered lower-performing employees.
